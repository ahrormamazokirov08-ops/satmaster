## 🎓 University Committee Defense: Anticipated Q&A & Rebuttals

Here are 4 tough academic questions university professors frequently ask, along with model defense responses:

### Q1: *"How does Uzbekistan balance its relations with Russia (within the CIS) without violating Western sanctions?"*
> **Model Defense Response:**  
> *"Uzbekistan adopts a policy of active regulatory compliance and transparent dialogue. The Central Bank of Uzbekistan and the Ministry of Investment coordinate directly with US OFAC and EU sanctions envoys. Uzbekistan maintains legitimate bilateral trade in humanitarian, agricultural, and consumer goods while strictly enforcing financial screening against sanctioned dual-use items. This demonstrates that economic partnership with Russia and strict compliance with international law can be harmonized without sacrificing sovereignty."*

### Q2: *"Why is the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan (CKU) railway only being built now after 27 years?"*
> **Model Defense Response:**  
> *"For nearly three decades, the project faced geopolitical hesitation regarding rail track gauge compatibility (Russian 1520mm vs. Chinese standard 1435mm) and financing models. The post-2016 breakthrough occurred because Uzbekistan proactively financed feasibility studies, brokered political compromises between Bishkek and Beijing, and demonstrated that the geopolitical disruption of northern transit corridors made an alternative southern route an existential necessity for global trade."*

### Q3: *"Can Central Asian integration succeed without a formal supranational body like the European Union?"*
> **Model Defense Response:**  
> *"Central Asian states deliberately avoid rigid supranational institutions because national sovereignty remains sacred following the Soviet collapse. Instead, Uzbekistan champions an ASEAN-style model of 'flexible multilateralism' and 'consensus over coercion' through the Consultative Meetings format. This prevents bureaucratic gridlock and avoids creating another forum where external powers could establish hegemony."*

### Q4: *"How does the Taliban's rule in Afghanistan affect the viability of the Trans-Afghan Railway?"*
> **Model Defense Response:**  
> *"While political recognition of the Taliban regime remains unresolved internationally, technical infrastructure cooperation is driven by practical necessity. Afghanistan's interim authorities have signed security guarantee protocols and assigned protection details for railway surveying teams. Both Pakistan and Uzbekistan recognize that providing Afghanistan with railway transit tariffs and trade integration is the single most effective antidote against extremist resurgence."*
